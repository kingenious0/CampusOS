# Methodology Slide: Design Science Research (DSR)

## Slide Title: Research Methodology: Design Science Research (DSR)

### 1. Problem Identification & Motivation
* **Context:** AAMUSTED Kumasi Campus lacks a dedicated digital navigation tool.
* **Problem:** Disorientation and lateness among students/visitors due to complex layouts.
* **Evidence:** Survey findings confirmed that 85 respondents face frequent wayfinding challenges.

### 2. Define Objectives of a Solution
* **Core Goal:** Create a unified digital platform for navigation and room identification.
* **Requirements:** Indoor-level accuracy, keyword search, and offline functionality (PWA).

### 3. Design & Development (The Artifact)
* **Tech Stack:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Mapbox GL JS, and GeoJSON.
* **Innovation:** Custom Dijkstra-based routing engine built for connectivity-independent navigation.

### 4. Demonstration
* **Scope:** Mapped 47 verified locations with detailed service/room descriptions.
* **Functionality:** Search -> Selection -> Route Generation -> Live Navigation HUD.

### 5. Evaluation
* **Testing:** System validation through performance benchmarking (Online OSRM vs. Offline Fallback).
* **Feedback:** User verification centered on search accuracy and routing reliability in peak hours.

### 6. Communication
* **Presentation:** Technical defense, comprehensive project report, and open-source documentation.
